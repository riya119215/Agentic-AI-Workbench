STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
DOCUMENT NO: SOP-TURB-IND-2026-V4
SECURITY CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED / OFFICIAL USE ONLY
ISSUING AUTHORITY: DIRECTORATE OF HEAVY INDUSTRIAL TURBOMACHINERY & QUALITY STANDARDS

1. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This document establishes mandatory operational limits, parametric tolerance envelopes, and inspection protocols for all Heavy Steam & Gas Turbine Power Generation Sets (Units 1 through 12).

2. CRITICAL PARAMETRIC TOLERANCE LIMITS
2.1 Bearing Vibration Envelopes (ISO 10816-3 Standard Alignment):
- Nominal/Safe Operational Zone: <= 2.80 mm/s RMS
- Advisory Warning Zone: 2.81 mm/s to 3.50 mm/s RMS (Continuous monitoring required)
- Critical Breach / Emergency Rejection Limit: > 3.50 mm/s RMS (Mandatory immediate rotor de-energization and overhaul)

2.2 Journal Bearing Temperature Tolerances:
- Nominal Range: 55.0 deg C to 75.0 deg C
- Upper Advisory Limit: 75.1 deg C to 85.0 deg C
- Critical Thermal Trip Limit: > 90.0 deg C (Thermal degradation risk to white metal Babbitt liner)

3. REGULATORY ACTION PROTOCOL
Whenever any field inspection report detects parameters exceeding the Critical Breach Thresholds (>3.50 mm/s vibration or >90.0 deg C temp):
- The Inspecting Officer must halt commercial operation within 4 hours.
- Issue Form QA-88: Official Note Sheet / Approval Memorandum for Emergency Bearing Overhaul and Sanction of Replacement Spares.
- The note must cite SOP-TURB-IND-2026-V4 Section 2.1 & Section 2.2 explicitly.
